Tips

1. Take your time when flipping cards and briefly note the image and its location in your mind, because rushing usually leads to forgetting and using more moves.

2. At the beginning of the game, focus on revealing as many different cards as possible to gather information rather than trying to force matches too early.

3. If you reveal a card and remember where its pair is located, match it immediately instead of flipping other cards first and risking that you forget.

4. When two cards don’t match, try to remember both of their positions because mismatched cards still give you useful information for future moves.

5. You should flip the cards using a consistent pattern, such as moving from left to right and top to bottom, so you don’t lose track of where each card is.

6. When you are near the end of the game, if only a few unknown cards remain close together, flipping them can quickly complete pairs because the chances of matching are higher.
